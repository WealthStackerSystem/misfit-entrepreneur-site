What to Do When You Don't Know What to Do

April 22, 2019

We've all been there — that point where we just don't know what to do. These are some of the greatest times in your life to grow. First, Take Inventory. Step back, take deep breaths and allow logic to help. What is the problem? Second, Focus on What You Can Control. Ask yourself what you can control and what you cannot. Narrow your focus on what you can do. Third, Ask Yourself Who Can Help and Why. Don't go it alone. Get help fast. Lastly, Create a Checklist. Write down your action steps and methodically go about doing them with a sense of urgency. The bigger the problems you can handle, the bigger your business and you can grow.
