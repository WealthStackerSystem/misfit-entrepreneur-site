The More You Do, The More You Can Do

May 19, 2018

My grandmother celebrated her 90th birthday recently. At 90, she golfs and swims almost every day. She dances at events weekly. As our family sat honoring her, one lesson really stood out. We got onto the topic of how she continues to be able to do all that she does. She gave a simple sentence for the answer: "The more you do, the more you can do." The more you do things in your life, the more of them you can do. It is conditioning. It works for everything — your mind, your body, your habits, your business. Think about your life and your work — what can you do more that will allow you to be even more successful? Pick one thing and get going today.
