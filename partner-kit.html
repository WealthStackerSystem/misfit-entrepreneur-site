The Misfit Advantage

01Host-read only. Always.

We do not run programmatic. We do not run pre-recorded spots. Dave reads every ad, in his own words, after using your product. Host-read ads convert at multiples of programmatic — and Misfit Nation can tell the difference.

02Your ad never expires.

Show notes placements are permanent. Episodes from 2017 still generate downloads. Your sponsorship keeps working after the check clears — for as long as MisfitEntrepreneur.com exists.

03Three slots. Three sponsors. Structurally.

Most shows stack six or seven advertisers per episode. We have three positions and we sell each one once. Your message doesn't compete for attention because there is nothing to compete with.

04Dave will not read an ad for a product he wouldn't use.

This is not a marketing line. It's the reason the ads convert. We reserve the right to decline sponsorship, and we exercise it.

05Real measurement.

Unique trackable URL, unique promo code, monthly reporting on downloads, geographic distribution, and conversion data. No black boxes.

Onboarding — How It Works

  1. Discovery call (30 min). We learn your ICP, your offer, and what a win looks like.
  2. Fit check. Dave uses the product. If it's not a fit, we tell you — and refer you elsewhere.
  3. Agreement + kickoff. Contract signed, first invoice issued.
  4. Copy & creative. You supply talking points; we write the read in Dave's voice and send for approval.
  5. Trackable assets built. Custom URL and promo code deployed across all channels.
  6. Launch. First spot goes live within 14 days of signature.
  7. Monthly reporting. Downloads, reach, clicks, and conversions.
  8. Quarterly optimization call. We adjust copy, offer, and placement based on what's working.
