I've Always Been a Misfit.

In high school, I was a star athlete — but I hung out with the musicians, the outcasts, those that were "a little crazy." I got into some trouble, just enough to teach me lessons and help me find myself along the way. I went to college, played football, got a couple of degrees. But from the time I was a kid, I always had the entrepreneurial itch — I started my first enterprise at age 5.

When I graduated, much to everyone's surprise (except my parents), I didn't get a job. I packed everything up and moved to Columbus, OH to start a health and wellness business. I failed miserably. I knew how to sell, but I wasn't prepared to run a business, lead people, or build a team.

"Broke and down — but not out. What looked like failure was actually the most important education of my life."

Building Companies. Finding the Pattern.

After rebuilding through a Fortune 500 role — sharpening every skill I lacked — I started investing in real estate, backed other businesses, and eventually went all-in on Grasp Technologies, a data and intelligence company in the travel industry. Grasp landed on the Inc 5000 and grew 50%+ year over year.

Today Dave is co-owner of TaxPlanIQ (fast-growing tax savings software), founder of 12 Minute Trading (trading education, programs & community for entrepreneurs who want systematic market income), GainStacker (automated SPY options directives with a 98.9% live win rate — execute in 10 minutes a day), and the WealthStacker System (probability-based wealth building designed to beat the S&P).
